# Fake Bread, Real God — Seedance 2.5 prompts (9:16, 40 s, two generations)

Shot at Lee Jimin's solo exhibition *FAKERY* (Chapter1 FOUND ARTIST #3, Doqu Seoul, 11–20 Sep 2026), where every loaf is sheer fabric fitted over a thin wire frame and the plates are paper.

YENA fills the left half, hand cupped by her mouth, one eyebrow up; BACCHUS in profile on the right half, goblet in hand, eyes on the bread beside him; the toast's brown crust edge enters only the far right strip of the frame at his shoulder height, turning. Endstate: both of them on the left, the crust edge turning at the right strip.

```
Stage 2 — 4–8s — LOW WIDE FROM BEHIND
Camera: low behind them at hip height, level, the horizon sits a few degrees off and never gets corrected. Both are seen from the BACK, backs to the camera, standing one metre to the left of the toast and looking across at it: the toast hangs at their right, bottom edge at their knees, top edge at the top of their heads, its whole surface plain bread crumb and brown crust. BACCHUS's head is turned to the right at eye level toward the bread; YENA turns her head sideways to him. He says slowly, lips moving: "Biggest bread I've ever seen. It'd go with my wine." Endstate: both backs to camera beside the toast, his head turning to her.

Stage 3 — 8–13s — SIDE TRACKING ALONG THE TABLE
Camera: chest height, tracking sideways with them. Hard cut to ROOM B; the tomato mobile cuts across the lens and the autofocus hunts before it lands on them. BACCHUS walks along the table and reaches his RIGHT hand to the baguettes; YENA lunges and grips his WRIST. His hand freezes 20 cm short. He stares at her, then at the bread. She says sharp, quick, her face readable beside his: "Don't touch!! It's not real. Fabric over wire, nothing inside."
Endstate: his hand held in the air, both faces to the baguettes.

Stage 4 — 13–20s — LOW INSERT, BAGUETTES FOREGROUND
Camera: table height; focus lands on the baguette before it racks to his face. Three baguettes fill the foreground, backlit, wire showing as they turn; behind them BACCHUS frozen staring, hand still up, YENA leaning in pointing at the wire without touching. Two seconds wordless, then he says quietly, lips moving, eyes on the bread: "Like my fruit bowl. Laid out, nobody eats it." YENA, off: "…Huh."
Endstate: baguette still turning, both faces visible, the frame still breathing.
```

```
[STYLE] EXACTLY 20 s, 9:16, MULTI-SHOT hard cuts, by a friend with a phone held in a hand — the framing of a phone held in a hand, never a selfie. Photoreal daylight. Rectilinear: straight lines stay straight, evenly bright into all four corners, only the light already in the room. Location sound runs continuously straight through every cut, unbroken. No music, no BGM.

[RULES] EXACT 2 CHARACTERS — NO DUPLICATES. CONTINUITY LOCK: both present first frame to last of every stage. The recording device stays behind the lens. Stages 5–7 within the same four metres of table; 8 at the same spot of the toast; 9 in the same corner; 10 inside the same window end. Walking, the camera travels with them. Her face stays readable in frame in Stages 5, 9, 10. Every hanging piece keeps turning. Nobody touches any artwork; at the toast his hands are clasped BEHIND his back, goblet in the left. Eyes ordinary, never brighter than skin. YENA: natural American English, young Korean-American. BACCHUS: slow, warm, low.

Stage 5 — 0–6s — MEDIUM, YENA TO CAMERA
Camera: handheld follow, walking backwards ahead of her, her whole face in frame; exposure dips as the window passes behind her. YENA walks along the table, hand cupped by her mouth; far behind BACCHUS, back turned, watches the turning tomato mobile. She whispers, lips moving, one glance back: "Between us… he's not a real god. Caravaggio put a bedsheet on a Roman street kid. Look at that tan."
Endstate: YENA mid-table, BACCHUS turning his head.

Stage 6 — 6–8s — OVER YENA'S SHOULDER
Camera: OTS, rack focus to him; the toast rack cuts across the lens in the foreground. BACCHUS has turned and looks past her at the camera, hand on his own shoulder, chin up. He says slowly, lips moving: "…He said toga." YENA presses her lips shut; he walks on down the table.
Endstate: he walks away, she follows.

Stage 7 — 8–10s — OVERHEAD, WORDLESS
Camera: straight down, drifting. The croissant plate and the tops of two heads — her bun, his wreath — pass beneath side by side. Footsteps only.
Endstate: both heads leaving the frame.

Stage 8 — 10–15s — LOW CLOSE AT THE TOAST
Camera: low at table height; focus lands on the butter cube before it finds his face. Bottom-left the toast, the butter cube turning above it; from the right BACCHUS lowers his face to 10 cm, hands behind his back, nostrils flaring; YENA soft behind, arms folded. Two seconds of breath, then he says low, lips moving: "It looks so good and I can't eat it. I've been hungry since 1597." Slow push-in to a tight close-up: a tear slides down his cheek, eyes on the butter, one sniff.
Endstate: the tear at his jaw, butter still turning.

Stage 9 — 15–17s — TWO-SHOT, YENA PROMINENT
Camera: chest-height two-shot, the sandwich mobile swinging across the lens edge. YENA beside him, hand on his shoulder, laughing while he wipes his eye; he glances at her. She says warm: "Real bakery. What do I get him?"
Endstate: YENA turning to walk, BACCHUS following.

Stage 10 — 17–20s — WIDE TO THE WINDOW, WHIP-PAN
Camera: wide handheld follow, the camera travels with them toward the glass wall, toasts swinging outside. YENA spins to face the camera walking backwards, thumb pointing back; BACCHUS beside her wiping his eye. She says fast, remembering: "Oh, by the way, the show ends today!" On her last word a whip-pan lands on the long table and holds one second.
Endstate: table wide, both small at the far end, mobiles turning.

[GLOBAL] Same people, clothing, goblet, light throughout. Only ONE butter cube. No subtitles. No music of any kind.
```
